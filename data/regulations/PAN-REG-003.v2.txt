PORT AUTHORITY OF NORTHGATE (PAN)
Regulation ID: PAN-REG-003
Version: 2
Authority: Port Authority of Northgate (PAN)
Port: Northgate
Topic: customs_compliance_hold
Effective Date: 2025-01-15
Supersedes: PAN-REG-003 v1

Section 1. Required documents. Cargo placed on a customs compliance hold must be supported by a commercial invoice, a certificate of origin, and a packing list.

Section 2. Submission deadline. The required documents must be submitted within 5 business days of the hold notice.

Section 3. Release. A hold is released within 2 business days of a complete submission.
