PORT AUTHORITY OF NORTHGATE (PAN)
Regulation ID: PAN-REG-009
Version: 1
Authority: Port Authority of Northgate (PAN)
Port: Northgate
Topic: vessel_detention
Effective Date: 2023-06-01

Section 1. Release after inspection. A vessel detained for a safety inspection may be released after the master submits the inspection remediation report.

Section 2. Detention charges. Detention charges accrue from the time of the detention notice.
