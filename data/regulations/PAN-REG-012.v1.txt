PORT AUTHORITY OF NORTHGATE (PAN)
Regulation ID: PAN-REG-012
Version: 1
Authority: Port Authority of Northgate (PAN)
Port: Northgate
Topic: port_closure
Effective Date: 2023-06-01

Section 1. Declaration. Port closures due to weather or security are declared by the Harbor Master through a public written notice stating the time the closure takes effect.

Section 2. Reopening. Reopening is announced by a separate public notice.
