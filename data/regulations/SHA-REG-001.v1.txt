SOUTHHAVEN HARBOR AUTHORITY (SHA)
Regulation ID: SHA-REG-001
Version: 1
Authority: Southhaven Harbor Authority (SHA)
Port: Southhaven
Topic: customs_compliance_hold
Effective Date: 2023-01-10

Section 1. Required documents. Cargo on a customs hold requires a commercial invoice and a bill of lading.

Section 2. Submission deadline. The required documents must be submitted within 7 business days of the hold notice.
