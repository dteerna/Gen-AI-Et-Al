## Governing clauses

- **force majeure**: MSA-GLCC-001#5.1: Force majeure. A party is excused from delay liability caused by force majeure events, including port closures ordered by a port authority, only if it gives written notice within 48 hours of the event and takes reasonable steps to mitigate the delay. Verbal notice does not satisfy this requirement.
- **notification deadline**: VA-ACME-2022#6.1: Incident notice. ACME Freight must notify GLCC of any damage, detention, or delay within 48 hours of discovery.

## Clauses that do not govern
